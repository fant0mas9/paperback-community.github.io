## Individual Extensions 
### Comick - How do I remove scans in other languages?

`Settings > Extensions > Comick > Language Settings`
Then select the language/s you wish to read. a tick will appear next to them.
You may wish to also toggle the "Filter Homepage Language" to on.

### Comick - How do I filter other scans out?

In the individual comic (eg Solo Leveling), open it so the chapter list is displaying, click on the three dots (...) then select Group Priority. Here you can sort the priorty of the scan groups by dragging on the hamburger (the three horizontal slashes). For example you could Official first followed by Flame Scans etc. 
If you then enable the setting "Hide Other Groups" it will only display the top group. It will display another group only if the first group does not have a chapter available. May need to exit out of the comic & go back into it for the chapters to refresh
